# Copyer v1.5.0 图标

按用户“重新设计一个符合风格的图标”的要求制作并接入本地版本。

- 两张叠合文件页表达复制；不放文字、照片、电脑或存储卡等细节。
- 白色玻璃底座呼应主界面，深灰轮廓保证浅色背景和小尺寸下能辨认。
- 保留 PNG 的透明通道；纯白、灰、深灰配色。
- 原图：项目根目录 `app_icon.png`，1254 × 1254 RGBA。
- Windows 图标：`app_icon.ico`，含 16、20、24、32、40、48、64、96、128、256px 十个尺寸。`tools/generate_icon.py` 只做格式转换和缩放，不再重画旧彩色图标。
- 预览及核验：`验收/图标修订/图标尺寸预览.png`、`图标核验.json`。预览中的 16–64px 均按真实像素显示；128px 一项缩至 64px，已在图中标注。
- 使用内置 **imagegen** 生成，并进行一次透明边缘修订；没有调用外部图像 API。交付资产已复制到项目内，运行和构建不依赖 Codex 缓存目录。

## 首次生成提示词

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: one production Windows desktop application icon for Copyer, a minimal photo and media importing utility. Generate a single square 1024x1024 app icon, not an icon sheet and not a presentation mockup.
Design: a softly rounded square tile of pure white translucent optical glass, viewed straight-on, flat orthographic composition. Center one very simple, bold copy symbol made from two overlapping rounded document panes: rear pane offset slightly up and left, front pane slightly down and right. The document silhouette should read instantly at 24px and 16px. Large balanced symbol, generous internal spacing, substantial dark neutral graphite contour rather than hairline strokes. The front document has an empty clean center: no letters, no lines of writing, no pictures, no arrow, no folded corner. Keep the mark to just the two overlapping shapes.
Materials: genuinely polished clear glass edges on the white tile and subtle glass thickness on the document panes, restrained optical refraction along curved edges, thin brilliant white specular highlights, soft gray internal reflections and gentle contact shadows. Quiet, precise, refined industrial design, not cartoon plastic, not inflated puffy clay. No repeated decorative concentric borders.
Palette: strictly neutral pure white, gray, charcoal. Absolutely no blue cast, cyan, purple, yellow, warm cream, rainbow or chromatic aberration.
Composition: single centered icon occupying about 90 percent of the canvas with a small balanced transparent safety margin. Completely transparent alpha background outside the rounded-square tile. No floor, no photographic scene, no cast shadow extending far beyond the icon, no checkerboard painted into the image. The interior of the tile is clean luminous white; do not include background grid lines, since these do not survive small taskbar sizes.
Constraints: no Apple logo, no Copyer wordmark, no brand names, no text, no watermark, no laptop, no SD card, no additional objects. Preserve a simple high-contrast central silhouette suitable for a real multi-resolution ICO file.
```

## 修订提示词

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit this single Copyer app icon for production alpha-edge cleanup only. Preserve the exact two overlapping document shapes, their positions and dimensions, the clear white glass rounded-square tile, graphite outlines, lighting, and overall neutral grayscale design. The ONLY requested correction is the icon's exterior: remove all stray white/gray speckles, noisy halos, rough fragments, and faint blue fringe outside the rounded-square silhouette. Create a mathematically clean smooth antialiased silhouette with an evenly transparent margin around the icon. Every area outside the smooth tile and a very restrained near-contact edge shadow must have true zero alpha. No grain, no mottled alpha, no debris. Purely neutral RGB channels; zero blue tint. Preserve transparency. Output one centered square PNG app icon on a truly transparent background. Do not add a solid white background or a drawn checkerboard, do not add text, do not change the central icon design.
```

生成工具实际返回 1254 × 1254，并带有极低透明度的边缘像素；格式导出保留原始 alpha，不声称执行了逐像素重绘。浅深背景合成预览及 ICO 多尺寸读取已核对。旧图标已在 Git 基线中保留，可从历史恢复。

减法审查：只保留双页这一核心符号，删去旧图标中的电脑、存储卡、照片与彩色箭头；没有新增应用设置或运行依赖。
